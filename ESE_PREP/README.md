## PLAIN-ENGLISH CHEAT SHEET (read this first)

1. Every RAI ESE paper is **50 marks, 2 hours, 5 questions × 10 marks**. All compulsory, but every question has internal choice ("attempt any two" / "any one").
2. The question structure is **identical every year**:
   - Q1 = short answers, attempt any two of three (5+5)
   - Q2 = attempt any one of two (10)
   - Q3 = attempt any one of two (10)
   - Q4 = attempt the following, both parts (5+5)
   - Q5 = attempt the following / any two (5+5)
3. The examiners **recycle question archetypes heavily**. In Maths, 8 of ~10 question types repeated across both available RAI papers. In H&N and Manufacturing the topic rotation is predictable module-by-module.
4. One module ≈ one question. 5 modules = 5 questions. Study module-wise, not chapter-wise.
5. **"Draw neat diagrams" is instruction #1 on every paper** — circuit sketches (H&N), diagrams (MP), figures (MoM) carry easy marks. A correct labelled diagram is often half the answer.
6. Show steps. Numerical answers without working get partial marks at best; formula + substitution + answer is the expected format.
