# ======== PAPER 1 — BEST PREDICTION ========

**Q1. Attempt any two:** *(2×5=10)*
(i) Classify engineering materials (metals/polymers/ceramics/composites); two applications of composites. **[BOTH — composites asked ND25; materials classification is the module opener asked KT-era]**
(ii) Differentiate edge vs screw dislocation; define Burgers vector. **[BOTH — dislocations asked in ND25 (stacking fault) and older KT papers]**
(iii) Short note: **fracture toughness**. **[ROT]**

**Q2. Attempt any one:** *(10)*
(i) A copper rod (Ø25, E = 100 GPa) and steel rod (Ø20, E = 200 GPa), each 1.5 m, jointly support 25 kN axially between rigid plates. Find stress & extension in each (equal strain). **[BOTH — copper+steel composite asked ND25 & ND21]**
(ii) Steel rod 30 mm Ø, 4 m, fixed between rigid walls; ΔT = +60°C, α = 12×10⁻⁶/°C, E = 210 GPa. Find stress; supports yield 0.5 mm total. **[KT/ROT — thermal stress classic]**

**Q3. Attempt any one:** *(10)*
(i) At a point: σx = 80 MPa(T), σy = 40 MPa(C), τxy = 30 MPa. Principal stresses + directions + max shear, analytically and by **Mohr's circle** (draw to scale). **[BOTH — Mohr asked 4/4 papers (ND25, KT25, Dec20, ND21)]**
(ii) Simply supported beam, span 5 m, UDL 10 kN/m; rectangular section 150×300 mm. Max bending stress + section modulus. **[BOTH — bending numerical asked ND25 & Dec20]**

**Q4. Attempt the following:** *(5+5=10)*
(a) Shaft transmits 30 kW at 200 rpm, Ø60 mm, 2 m, G = 84 GPa. Max shear stress + angle of twist. **[BOTH — torsion power numerical asked Dec20 & ND21-era]**
(b) Differentiate solid vs hollow shaft — efficiency argument. **[ROT]**

**Q5. Attempt the following:** *(5+5=10)*
(a) Fatigue: definition, mechanism, testing, S-N curve, influencing factors. **[BOTH — ND25 asked fatigue+S-N as both halves]**
(b) Types of corrosion with examples; two prevention methods. **[BOTH — corrosion asked ND25]**

---KEY P1---
Q2(i): equal strain → σs = 2σc. σc(Ac + 2As) = 25e3, Ac = 490.9, As = 314.2 mm² → σc = 25000/1119.2 = 22.3 MPa, σs = 44.7 MPa; δ = σcL/Ec = 0.335 mm. (verified)
Q2(ii): σ = EαΔT = 210000·12e−6·60 = 151.2 MPa (C); with yield 0.5 mm: net = 2.88−0.5 = 2.38 → σ = 124.9 MPa.
Q3(i): σ1,2 = 20±67.08 = 87.08, −47.08; τmax = 67.08; θp = ½ tan⁻¹(60/120) = 13.28°.
Q3(ii): M = 31.25 kNm; I = 3.375×10⁸ mm⁴; σ = 13.89 MPa; Z = 2.25×10⁶ mm³.
Q4(a): T = 1432.4 Nm; τ = 33.8 MPa; θ = 0.0268 rad = 1.54°. (verified)
Q5: theory per module-5 notes.

# ======== PAPER 2 — GOOD PREDICTION ========

**Q1. Attempt any two:** *(2×5=10)*
(i) Point defects: vacancy, interstitial, impurity — explain with sketches. **[ROT — ND25 asked stacking fault (surface); point defects are the neighbour]**
(ii) Define creep; draw and label the creep curve; three stages. **[BOTH-adjacent — creep asked KT25; ND25 fatigue — the pair rotates]**

**Q2. Attempt any one:** *(10)*
(i) Bar with mid-length load: two portions in series — steel AC (2 m, A=400 mm²) + copper CB (2 m, 10 kN at C... construct: total 4 m, load at junction). Find reactions, stresses, extension of each. **[ROT — series variant of the composite archetype]**
(ii) Strain energy & resilience: bar 40×40 mm, 2 m, 128 kN, E = 200 GPa. Find elongation, strain energy, proof resilience, modulus of resilience. **[NEW — strain energy never asked in RAI era; standard module-2 item]**

**Q2-alt (either slot):** At a point σx = σ, σy = −σ/2, τ = σ/4 type Mohr variant — principal stresses. **[ROT]**

**Q3. Attempt any one:** *(10)*
(i) Cantilever 2 m, end load 20 kN, 100×200 mm section. Max bending stress; compare if section rotated flat (200 wide × 100 deep). **[ROT — ND25 asked 150×300 SS beam; cantilever + orientation twist]**
(ii) T-section beam (flange 100×20, web 20×80), simply supported 4 m, central point load 20 kN. Find NA, I, max tensile & compressive bending stresses. **[BOTH — T-section bending asked Dec20 & ND21]**

**Q4. Attempt the following:** *(5+5=10)*
(a) **Macaulay's method**: SS beam span L with point load W at midspan — slope & deflection at midspan (derive δ = WL³/48EI). **[BOTH — Macaulay asked ND25, Dec20, ND21]**
(b) Hollow shaft OD 100, ID 50, τmax = 60 MPa, 240 rpm. Power transmitted. **[ROT — torsion rotation to hollow]**

**Q5. Attempt the following:** *(5+5=10)*
(a) Griffith's theory of brittle fracture; Orowan's modification. **[ROT — 5.1 theory pair]**
(b) Strain hardening: dislocation explanation + recrystallization annealing. **[ROT]**

---KEY P2---
Q2(i): series: σ = P/A each; δ_total = PL/(A_s E_s) + PL/(A_c E_c) with reactions split by rigidity if statically indeterminate — practice the rigid-bar variant.
Q2(ii): δ = 0.8 mm; U = P²L/2AE = 51.2 J; proof resilience = U at elastic limit; modulus = U/V.
Q3(i): M = 40 kNm; upright I = 6.667e7, y=100 → σ = 60 MPa; flat I = 1.667e7, y=50 → σ = 120 MPa (2× worse). (verified)
Q3(ii): ȳ = (2000·90+1600·40)/3600 = 67.8 mm from bottom; I_NA = 3.14×10⁶ mm⁴; M = WL/4 = 20 kN·m → σ_top = 20e6·32.2/3.14e6 = 205 MPa (C), σ_bot = 20e6·67.8/3.14e6 = 431 MPa (T). (verified)
Q4(a): standard result δ = WL³/48EI, θ = WL²/16EI.
Q4(b): J = 9.204e6 mm⁴; T = 11.04 kNm; P = 277.6 kW.
