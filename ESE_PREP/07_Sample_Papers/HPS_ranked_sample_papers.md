# ======== PAPER 1 — BEST PREDICTION ========

**Q1. Attempt any two:** *(2×5=10)*
(i) Short note on any ONE pressure sensor (pool: Bourdon / piezoelectric / diaphragm / manometer — one was asked in EACH real paper). **[BOTH]**
(ii) State Pascal's law with equation + hydraulic-lift numerical: pistons 40 cm and 4 cm, 20 N on small → force on large. **[BOTH — Pascal asked in both (ND25 law+apps, MJ25 lift numerical)]**
(iii) Compare hydraulic vs pneumatic systems (≥5 points). **[BOTH-adjacent — comparison asked ND25; both papers' Q1 draw from this trio]**

**Q2. Attempt any one:** *(10)*
(i) Explain **gear pump and vane pump**: construction, working, adv/disadv with diagrams. **[BOTH — pumps asked in both (MJ25 motors+compressors-adjacent, ND25 piston+plunger)]**
(ii) **Accumulators**: function, types, bladder-type construction & working. **[ROT — MJ25-era candidate; ND25 asked intensifier → rotate]**

**Q3. Attempt any one:** *(10)*
(i) **Sequence valve** + draw the two-cylinder sequencing circuit (clamp → press). **[BOTH — ND25 asked sequence; MJ25 counterbalance → same family, both windows]**
(ii) **Pressure-compensated FCV** + meter-in/meter-out circuits: draw, explain, when each preferred. **[BOTH — ND25 asked exactly this]**

**Q4. Attempt the following:** *(5+5=10)*
(i) Draw: **indirect control of a double-acting cylinder** (pilot-operated 3/2 or 5/2 + push-button). Name all components. **[BOTH — Q4 pneumatic circuit in both papers (MJ25 oscillatory, ND25 indirect)]**
(ii) **Shuttle valve** — symbol, construction, application (OR logic). **[BOTH — ND25 asked shuttle; MJ25 time-delay → valve-short slot confirmed]**

**Q5. Attempt the following:** *(5+5=10)*
(i) Role of the **power supply** in electro-pneumatic systems + relay construction/working. **[BOTH — ND25 power supply, MJ25 relay]**
(ii) Electro-pneumatic circuit: **OR logic** (two parallel push buttons) for single-acting cylinder — OR — **auto-return** double-acting cylinder with limit switch. **[BOTH — logic/auto-return circuit in both papers]**

---KEY P1---
Q1(ii): F = 20×(40/4)² = 2000 N.
Q2(i): gear 20–175 bar noisy cheap; vane quieter, cam ring, variable displacement.
Q3(i): sequence = normally-closed pressure valve with bypass/check; circuit: pump → clamp cyl → sequence valve (set below system relief, above clamp pressure) → press cyl.
Q3(ii): compensator keeps ΔP across throttle constant → flow ∝ throttle area only, load-independent; meter-in for resistive loads, meter-out for overrunning.
Q4(i): PB 3/2 → pilot of 5/2 DCV → cylinder; indirect lets small valve switch big flow.
Q4(ii): shuttle picks higher of two inputs; safety dual-station control.
Q5(i): PSU converts 230V AC → 24V DC for solenoids; relay = coil + NO/NC contacts, isolation & amplification.
Q5(ii): two 3/2 NO PBs in parallel → either energizes relay → relay NO contact drives solenoid Y1 → 5/2 shifts → cylinder extends.
